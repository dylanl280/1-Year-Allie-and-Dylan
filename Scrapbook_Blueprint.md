# One Year Together — Scrapbook Blueprint
*For Allie & Dylan · Anniversary June 29, 2025 · Theme: romantic & elegant, soft purple with peonies & tulips*

**Structure: one photo per page (52 pages) + cover + closing.**

- **Cover** — "One Year Together — Allie & Dylan", peony/tulip florals, purple palette.
- **Pages 1–52** — one photo each, in chronological order (see `Scrapbook_Timeline.csv`). Each page has:
  - the photo, elegantly framed
  - a printed **date** stamp
  - a printed **location** label
  - a **blank caption box** for Dylan to fill in
- **Closing** — "Here's to forever / Year One, and counting", florals.

Page order follows the master timeline, May 23 2025 → Jun 14 2026. Consistent layout across all pages; left/right photo alignment can alternate for visual rhythm.
